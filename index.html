I'm a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, originally from Velo, Greece. I work in the PCL group under the supervision of George Tzimpragos. My research focuses on computer architecture for emerging hardware platforms — particularly superconducting computing — and on designing systems for ML inference under extreme resource constraints. I'm also interested in the EDA tools that support these efforts.
